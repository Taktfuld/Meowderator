# 🐾 Meowderator

All-in-one Discord moderation, anti-nuke and utility bot. Free to use, made with love (and too many cat puns).

## 💛 Before you add me

Meowderator is a free project, so please read this first:

- **Use it at your own risk.** I do my best to keep it running smoothly, but I can't make any guarantees.
- **You're in charge of your server.** You pick the settings and permissions, so what the bot does there is your responsibility. If something goes wrong, like a raid, lost channels or roles, or a wrong ban, I can't be held responsible.
- **Things can break.** Bugs, downtime, Discord changes and host outages happen, and I can't promise to fix everything.
- **Please keep backups.** The cat tries hard, but it can't bring back what's lost.
- **Your data:** everything the bot stores is listed in the Privacy Policy. If it ever collects something that isn't listed there, that's probably a bug. Let me know, and it'll be fixed and the data removed. Discord, Roblox and other services collect their own data under their own rules.
- **Be nice.** Using the bot to break rules or hurt others gets you blacklisted.

> Thanks for using Meowderator! Have fun and stay safe. 🐈
