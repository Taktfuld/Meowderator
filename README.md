# 🐾 Meowderator

All-in-one Discord moderation, anti-nuke, and utility bot. Free to use, made with love (and too many cat puns).

## 💛 Before you add me

Meowderator is a free project, so please read this first:

- **Use it at your own risk.** I do my best to keep it running smoothly, but I can't make any guarantees.
- **You're in charge of your server.** You choose the settings and permissions, so what the bot does in your server is your responsibility. If something goes wrong, such as a raid, lost channels or roles, or an incorrect ban, I can't be held responsible.
- **Things can break.** Bugs, downtime, Discord changes, and host outages happen. I can't promise to fix everything.
- **Please keep backups.** The cat tries hard, but it can't bring back what's lost.
- **Your data.** Everything the bot stores is listed on the bot under **Data**. If it ever collects something that isn't listed there, that's probably a bug. Discord, Roblox, and other services collect their own data under their own rules.
- **Be nice.** Using the bot to break rules or hurt others may get you blacklisted.
- **Important.** I don't read my DMs or emails often because I receive a lot. Please don't expect a reply.

> Thanks for using Meowderator! Have fun and stay safe. 🐈
